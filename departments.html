Comprehensive Medical Departments

Our hospital offers a wide range of specialized departments equipped with advanced technology and experienced doctors to provide the highest quality healthcare services.Our hospital offers a wide range of specialized medical departments including Cardiology, Orthopedics, Neurology, Pediatrics, Gynecology & Obstetrics, General Medicine, Emergency & Trauma Care, Radiology, Dermatology, and ENT. Each department is equipped with advanced technology and experienced medical professionals to provide accurate diagnosis, effective treatment, and compassionate patient care. From heart and brain disorders to child healthcare, women’s health, skin treatments, emergency services, and diagnostic imaging, we ensure comprehensive and quality healthcare services under one roof with a strong commitment to patient safety and excellence.

General Medicine

General Medicine department provides complete medical care for adults, focusing on diagnosis and management of various health conditions.The General Medicine department (or Internal Medicine) provides comprehensive, non-surgical healthcare for adults, serving as the primary point of contact for diagnosing, treating, and preventing acute and chronic illnesses. Staffed by internists or physicians, this department manages complex, multi-system diseases, and coordinates care for both inpatients and outpatients.

This department treats chronic diseases like diabetes, hypertension, infections, fever, respiratory disorders, and lifestyle-related illnesses. Our experienced physicians ensure accurate diagnosis and long-term care.

Key Aspects of General Medicine

  • Clinical Care: Covers a broad spectrum of health issues, including infections, metabolic diseases, cardiovascular conditions, and complex diagnostic challenges.
  • Preventive Medicine: Focuses on disease prevention, health education, and regular health check-ups to promote long-term wellness.
  • Diagnostic Expertise: Utilizes physical examinations, laboratory tests, and imaging techniques to diagnose illnesses, including complex multi-system conditions.
  • Inpatient & Outpatient Care: Manages patients through OPD services, emergency care, and inpatient (IPD) hospital management.
  • Sub-specialties: Includes Gastroenterology, Cardiology, Endocrinology, and Hepatology for specialized medical care.
  • Academic Role: Acts as a cornerstone of medical education, training undergraduate and postgraduate students through clinical teaching, lectures, and research.

Common Conditions Treated

  • Infectious diseases (Flu, Pneumonia, Tropical diseases)
  • Metabolic disorders (Diabetes, Thyroid disorders)
  • Cardiovascular conditions (Hypertension)
  • Respiratory disorders
  • Chroni

Neurology

Neurology department deals with disorders of the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system.A neurology department specializes in the diagnosis, treatment, and management of disorders affecting the brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves, and muscles. These departments offer comprehensive care, ranging from emergency stroke treatment to chronic care for epilepsy, dementia, Parkinson's disease, and migraines. Services typically include advanced diagnostics like MRI/CT scans, EEG, and EMG, along with specialized clinics for rehabilitation and, in some cases, neurosurgery and deep brain stimulation.

Treatment includes epilepsy, stroke, migraine, Parkinson’s disease, nerve disorders, and neurological rehabilitation using advanced technology.

Key Aspects of Neurology Department

  • Core Conditions Treated: Stroke, Epilepsy, Parkinson’s disease, Dementia (Alzheimer’s), Multiple Sclerosis, Headaches and Migraines, Neuropathy, Meningitis, and Muscular Dystrophy.
  • Emergency Services: Provides specialized and rapid care for acute neurological events, including stroke thrombolysis, mechanical thrombectomy, and management of status epilepticus.
  • Specialized Clinics: Dedicated clinics for Movement Disorders, Memory Disorders, Neuropsychiatry, Sleep Disorders, and Pediatric Neurology.
  • Diagnostics and Technology: Advanced diagnostic facilities including 3T MRI, PET-CT, Neuro-Interventional Laboratory, Electromyography (EMG), Electroencephalography (EEG), and Nerve Conduction Studies.
  • Specialized Staff: Multidisciplinary team of Neurologists, Neurosurgeons, Neurophysiologists, Neurointensivists, and Rehabilitation Specialists including speech and physical therapists.

Common Subspecialties & Specialized Services

  • Stroke & Neurovascular Care: Dedicated, high-level, 24/7 care for acute brain attacks.
  • Epilepsy Management: Advanced seizure monitoring and surgical treatment options.
  • Movement Disorders: Botox therapy, Botox injections, and Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS) for Parkinson’s disease and tremors.
  • Neuro-Rehabilitation: Focused rehabilitation programs aimed at improving quality of life for stroke and Parkinson’s patients.

Diagnostic Procedures

  • Imaging: CT Scan, MRI, and Magnetic Resonance Angiography (MRA).
  • Electro-diagnosis: EEG (brain wave analysis), EMG (muscle activity), and NCS (nerve conductivity studies).
  • Procedures: Lumbar puncture (spinal tap) for cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) analysis.

Pathology

Pathology department provides accurate laboratory testing for disease diagnosis.The Department of Pathology is a critical hospital unit focused on diagnosing diseases through the analysis of tissues, blood, and body fluids, acting as the backbone of clinical medicine. It comprises sub-specialties like histopathology, cytopathology, and hematology, providing essential, timely, and reliable reports for patient treatment, research, and education.

Services include blood tests, urine analysis, biopsy examination, histopathology, and preventive health check-up reports.

Key Aspects of Pathology Department

  • Core Services: Diagnostic services include Histopathology (tissue analysis), Cytopathology (cell analysis), Hematology (blood disorder evaluation), Microbiology, and Clinical Pathology.
  • Specialized Functions: Advanced services such as blood banking, frozen section analysis for rapid intraoperative diagnosis, and molecular diagnostics for precise disease identification.
  • Key Personnel: The department is staffed by qualified Pathologists, laboratory technicians, and resident doctors specializing in disease diagnosis.
  • Key Facilities: Equipped with modern microscopes, microtomes, automatic cell counters, and fully computerized laboratory information systems.
  • Academic & Research Role: In teaching hospitals, the department conducts undergraduate and postgraduate training, follows competency-based education, and maintains a museum of pathological specimens.
  • Accreditation & Quality Standards: Operates in compliance with NABL and NABH standards to ensure accurate, reliable, and timely diagnostic results.

The Pathology Department plays a critical role in bridging laboratory science with clinical practice by translating diagnostic findings into effective patient management plans.

Cardiology

Cardiology department focuses on heart and blood vessel-related diseases.A cardiology department is a specialized medical unit focused on diagnosing, treating, and preventing cardiovascular diseases, including coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, and heart failure. These departments offer comprehensive care through non-invasive diagnostics (ECG, echo) and interventional procedures (angioplasty, stenting) in dedicated labs. Key teams include cardiologists, specialized nurses, and surgeons who manage acute and chronic heart condition

Treatment includes heart attack care, ECG, angiography, hypertension management, and preventive cardiac programs.

Key Aspects of Cardiology Department

  • Diagnostics: Non-invasive tests such as Echocardiography, Electrocardiogram (ECG), Treadmill Test (TMT), and Holter monitoring, along with advanced imaging including Cardiac MRI and 128-slice CT scans.
  • Interventional Cardiology: Minimally invasive procedures performed in advanced Catheterization Laboratories (Cath Labs), including coronary angiography, angioplasty, stenting, and pacemaker implantation.
  • Electrophysiology: Diagnosis and treatment of heart rhythm disorders using advanced cardiac mapping systems and catheter ablation techniques.
  • Structural Heart Disease: Non-surgical and minimally invasive repair of structural heart defects such as ASD, VSD, and PDA closures.
  • Emergency Cardiac Care: Provides round-the-clock (24/7) emergency services for acute cardiac conditions including heart attacks (Acute Coronary Syndrome).

Subspecialties & Cardiac Care Team

  • General Cardiologists: Manage overall heart health and long-term cardiac conditions.
  • Interventional Cardiologists: Specialize in catheter-based cardiac procedures.
  • Electrophysiologists: Focus on diagnosis and treatment of electrical and rhythm-related heart disorders.

Orthopaedic

Orthopaedic department treats bone, joint, and muscle disorders. Orthopedics is a medical specialty focused on the musculoskeletal system— bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, tendons, and nerves—using surgical and non-surgical methods to treat injuries (like fractures, sports injuries) and conditions (like arthritis, deformities, infections, tumors) to restore movement and function for all ages.

Orthopedic surgeons diagnose, treat, and prevent disorders affecting the body's structure and mobility, often working with a team of therapists and specialists for comprehensive care, from broken bones to joint replacements.

Services include fracture management, joint replacement, arthritis treatment, sports injury care, and physiotherapy support.

Key Aspects of Orthopaedic Department

  • Bones: Diagnosis and treatment of fractures, bone infections, tumors, and congenital or acquired deformities.
  • Joints: Management of joint-related conditions including osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, joint injuries, and joint replacement surgeries.
  • Muscles, Ligaments & Tendons: Treatment of tears, strains, sports injuries, and chronic musculoskeletal conditions.
  • Nerves: Care for nerve-related musculoskeletal disorders such as carpal tunnel syndrome and nerve compression injuries.

What Orthopaedic Specialists Do

  • Diagnosis: Identify the root cause of musculoskeletal pain, injury, or functional impairment.
  • Treatment:
    • Non-Surgical: Medications, physiotherapy, injections, braces, and lifestyle guidance.
    • Surgical: Arthroscopy, joint replacement, fracture fixation, and reconstructive surgery.
  • Rehabilitation: Assist patients in regaining strength, mobility, and functional independence after injury or surgery.

Key Areas of Focus

  • Sports Injuries
  • Trauma and Fracture Management
  • Spine Disorders
  • Joint Replacement Surgery (Hip and Knee)
  • Pediatric Orthopaedics (Congenital & Developmental Conditions)

Dermatology

Dermatology department specializes in skin, hair, and nail care.A dermatology department specializes in diagnosing, treating, and preventing diseases of the skin, hair, nails, and mucous membranes, as well as managing cosmetic, aging, and cancerous conditions. These departments offer medical, surgical (dermatosurgery), and cosmetic services, including laser treatments, phototherapy, skin biopsies, and specialized clinics for conditions like psoriasis and vitiligo.

Treatments include acne, allergies, pigmentation, hair loss treatment, cosmetic dermatology, and laser therapy.

Key Aspects of Dermatology Department

  • Core Services: Treatment of acne, eczema, skin infections (viral, bacterial, fungal), hair loss, skin cancer, and autoimmune skin diseases.
  • Dermatosurgery: Specialized surgical procedures including mole removal, excision of skin cancers, cyst removal, and other minor dermatological surgeries.
  • Cosmetic Dermatology: Advanced aesthetic services such as chemical peels, Botox and filler injections, laser hair removal, skin rejuvenation, and tattoo removal.
  • Diagnostics: Accurate diagnosis using skin biopsy, dermatoscopy, patch testing, and microbiological investigations including smears and cultures.
  • Specialty Clinics: Dedicated clinics for chronic and complex skin conditions such as psoriasis, vitiligo, and Hansen’s disease (leprosy).
  • Subspecialties: Includes Pediatric Dermatology (children), Venereology (sexually transmitted infections), and Dermatopathology.

Common Procedures & Equipment

  • Phototherapy: NBUVB, PUVA, and targeted spot therapy for long-term management of chronic skin disorders.
  • Laser Therapy: Used for pigmentation disorders, vascular lesions, acne scars, and overall skin rejuvenation.

It bridges clinical care with food service management, offering customized diets for inpatients (including enteral/parenteral nutrition) and outpatients.

Dental

Dental department offers comprehensive oral healthcare.Dentistry is the branch of medicine focused on the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of oral cavity diseases, including teeth, gums, and the jaw. Proper dental care, involving twice-daily brushing and daily flossing, is crucial for preventing cavities, gum disease, and serious systemic health issues, such as heart disease.

Services include dental cleaning, fillings, root canal, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, and oral surgery.

Key Aspects of Dental Care

  • Preventive Care: Regular dental checkups (ideally every six months) are essential to prevent tooth decay, gum disease, and to identify oral health issues at an early stage.
  • Common Conditions: Management of dental problems such as tooth decay (cavities), plaque and tartar buildup, gingivitis (inflamed gums), and periodontitis (advanced gum disease).
  • Dental Treatments: Comprehensive treatments including fillings for cavities, root canal therapy for infections, scaling and polishing for professional cleaning, and crowns for restoring damaged teeth.
  • Oral Health & Systemic Link: Oral health acts as a window to overall physical health, as many systemic diseases may present early signs within the oral cavity.
